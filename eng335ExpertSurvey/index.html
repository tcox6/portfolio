Timetable Optimisation Project - Student Survey

This survey aims to inform the evaluation metrics that I use for a timetabling optimisation project. I plan to aggregate the answers from this form and feed them to a fuzzy expert system, which will form the basis for my objective function. In this instance, you (student) are an 'expert', but there's no need to take it too seriously :)

For each of the following timetables, rate each individual day as 'Good' 'Neutral', or 'Bad', and provide a rating for the overall timetable.
